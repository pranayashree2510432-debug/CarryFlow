# 3–4 minute demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — Problem

“Applying across portals means repeating the same details, while each portal names those details differently. CarryFlow keeps the source and meaning attached as the work moves.”

## 0:30–1:00 — Start

Click **Start demo**. Show the Student Profile and the Passport facts list. Point out source labels, versions, verification, and permitted origins.

## 1:00–1:40 — Cross-site handoff

Open Northstar using `02`, select **Prepare this portal**, and show “Candidate name,” “Academic background,” and “Expected completion” receipts. Select **Fill approved fields** and point out that the verified values appear in the actual local application form.

Open Summit using `03`, prepare it, and review its different labels. Highlight “Highest qualification” and “Passing year.” The generic “Location” field remains unfilled because it could mean either current or permanent address.

## 1:40–2:10 — Conflict

Click **Introduce phone conflict**. Show both versions, their sources, and the unresolved marker. Choose the correct version. Explain that old history remains available and future mappings use the resolved version.

## 2:10–2:50 — Recovery

Fill the approved Summit fields, then click **Interrupt & resume**. CarryFlow reads visible form values and compares them with saved action receipts. Show the recovery timeline entry and actions view. Emphasize that it does not submit or blindly replay an uncertain action.

## 2:50–3:30 — Approval and close

Click **Prepare submission** to show a prepared action. Only after explicit approval click **Approve demo submission**; the local site records a demo confirmation and sends nothing externally. Close with: “Websites may be disconnected. Your workflow doesn't have to be.”
